# Concepts

These pages define the words the product uses. A vault is what a business offers. A share is what a holder receives. The name and the symbol are what that holder sees.

| Concept | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| [The vault](/concepts/vault) | What gets created, and the asset it holds |
| [Shares](/concepts/shares) | What a deposit gives the holder, and how withdraw and redeem differ |
| [Name and symbol](/concepts/name-and-symbol) | What the business chooses, and what stays fixed |
| [Console and registry](/concepts/console) | Which system signs, which system checks, and which system stores the record |
