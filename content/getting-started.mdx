# Getting started

This guide helps you orient around PineryZ and VaultOS while the platform is still under development.

## 1. Read the company context

Start with the [PineryZ website](https://pineryz.com) for positioning and product intent. PineryZ is the company brand. VaultOS is the planned flagship product under that brand.

## 2. Review VaultOS

See [VaultOS](/vaultos) for the current product direction: white-label on-chain vault infrastructure that businesses can present under their own name and symbol.

## 3. Talk to the team

If you are evaluating VaultOS for a business use case, reach out through [Contact](https://pineryz.com/contact) or email [info@pineryz.com](mailto:info@pineryz.com).

## Notes on status

Treat everything here as product vision and planning material unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Do not assume production availability, audits, or customer deployments.
