# Getting started

<Callout type="important">
  Technical demonstration on Ethereum Sepolia. vUSD is a test token and has no market value.
</Callout>

Use this page to open the VaultOS demonstration, or to decide which doc to read first.

## Before you open the console

You need:

- A browser wallet that can add Ethereum Sepolia (chain id `11155111`)
- A small amount of Sepolia ETH in that wallet, to pay gas

VaultOS does not supply Sepolia ETH and does not hold the wallet key. The asset inside the demonstration, vUSD, is minted from the console. vUSD is a test token. It has no market value.

## Open the demonstration

1. Go to [demo.pineryz.com](https://demo.pineryz.com).
2. Connect the wallet and switch it to Ethereum Sepolia.
3. Follow [Create a vault](/vaultos/create-a-vault) to mint vUSD, create a vault, and wait for registration.
4. Follow [Use a vault](/vaultos/use-a-vault) to deposit, withdraw, or redeem.

The console navigation is Overview, My Vaults, Create Vault, Transaction History, and Settings. Settings lists the network, the factory, and the test asset.
