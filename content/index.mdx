# PineryZ Docs

PineryZ builds on-chain vault infrastructure for businesses that want to launch and manage their own vault products. VaultOS is the planned flagship. The public example of that work is a technical demonstration on Ethereum Sepolia.

## What the demonstration is

The console at [demo.pineryz.com](https://demo.pineryz.com) lets a wallet on Ethereum Sepolia mint demonstration vUSD, create a vault under a chosen name and symbol, and deposit or withdraw that asset. The registry records a vault after it verifies the creation transaction. The wallet signs every transaction. VaultOS does not receive the private key.
