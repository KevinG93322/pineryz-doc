# VaultOS

PineryZ builds the vault a business offers under its own name. The holder of the share sees that name. PineryZ supplies the factory, the registry, and the means to operate the vault.

The foundation for that vault is already running. These pages describe the product built on it: what a business gets, how a vault works, and the work still to do.

<Callout type="important">
  These pages describe the product being built. They state no release date, and they are not an offer of securities.
</Callout>

## What a business gets

A way to launch a vault, configure it, account for it, and monitor it, without assembling the factory, the registry, and the operator tools itself.

The business chooses the name and the symbol. Its clients see that name. PineryZ stays underneath.

## What a holder sees

A share token in the business’s name and symbol. The share is an ERC-4626 claim on the assets in the vault. The wallet signs every movement. The product does not take the private key.

## The first release

The first release is an operable vault:

- An asset whose supply the product controls
- Configuration a business can set after creation, including fees, caps, and a pause
- An operator role for that configuration
- A ledger of deposits, withdrawals, and redemptions
- A documented API a business system can call

The console is one client of that API. The work after this release is [the build](/vaultos/build): strategies, the controls a firm needs for its own clients, real-world assets, and a surface for other builders.

## Who the product is built for

| Segment | The vault they would offer |
| --- | --- |
| Real-world asset platforms | A vault around a tokenized asset, once that part of the product is built |
| Asset managers | A vault in their name, which they operate |
| Decentralized finance, fintech, and Web3 | A vault without assembling the factory, the registry, and the operator tools |

No segment is chosen as the first. No customer is named. Choosing a segment can pull later work forward. It does not skip the operable vault.

## Rules the product keeps

- The wallet signs. The product does not take the private key.
- A vault is listed only after its creation transaction checks out.
- The share stays ERC-4626. Later work sits behind that claim.
