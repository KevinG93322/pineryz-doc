# VaultOS

<Callout type="important">
  Technical demonstration on Ethereum Sepolia. The vault foundation is what you can exercise today.
</Callout>

VaultOS is PineryZ’s planned flagship: white-label on-chain vault infrastructure for businesses. A business can create a vault under its own name and symbol, and the console registers that vault only after the creation transaction checks out.

## Intent

Give a business a configurable way to launch and manage an on-chain vault without assembling the contracts, the registry, and the console alone.

The business chooses the vault name and the share symbol. Its customers see that name. PineryZ stays underneath.

The longer direction is a yield platform on the same foundation. The demonstration stops at the vault. It does not run yield. See [Status and limits](/status).

## Who it is for

The intended customers are businesses that want to offer their own on-chain vault products:

- Real-world asset platforms
- Digital asset managers
- Decentralized finance companies
- Fintech companies
- Web3 businesses

These are target segments. The docs do not list customers or design partners.
