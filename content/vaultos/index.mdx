# VaultOS

VaultOS is PineryZ’s planned flagship product: white-label on-chain vault infrastructure for businesses.

## Intent

Give businesses a configurable way to develop, launch, and manage on-chain financial vaults — and related products on the same foundation — without assembling that infrastructure alone.

The business chooses the vault name and symbol. Its customers see the business. PineryZ stays underneath.

## Status

VaultOS is under development / product vision. These docs describe direction, not a generally available production system.

## Audience

Potential customers under consideration include:

- Real-world asset (RWA) platforms
- Digital asset managers
- DeFi companies
- Fintech companies
- Web3 businesses
