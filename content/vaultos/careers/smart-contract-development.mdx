# Smart contract development

You will design the contracts for the vault a business offers under its own name. The factory and the ERC-4626 vault are already running. This role extends them into the first release.

The opening work is a controlled asset, configuration on the vault, and an operator who can set a fee, a cap, or a pause without taking holder funds. The share stays ERC-4626, including the guard against a deposit that would mint zero shares. The wallet signs every movement. The product does not take the private key.

A strategy module, separated roles, and a new asset model come later, on [The build](/vaultos/build). They are not the first contracts this role writes.

## Responsibilities

- Extend the vault and the factory so a business can offer one controlled asset under its own name and symbol.
- Add configuration for fees, caps, and a pause, with share accounting left on the ERC-4626 standard.
- Keep moving assets as a signature by the holder. The operator changes configuration.
- Keep the registration checks honest: a vault is listed only when its creation transaction matches the factory’s approved asset and code.
- Write the tests that exercise creation, deposit, withdrawal, redemption, and the new configuration.
- Review the vault, the factory, and those checks before an asset of value is accepted.

## Requirements

- Experience with Solidity and EVM smart contracts.
- Familiarity with ERC-20 and ERC-4626.
- Ability to explain a design clearly, in writing and in review.
- Comfort reading an existing codebase and extending the same pattern.
- Care for tests, and for stating what a contract does.
