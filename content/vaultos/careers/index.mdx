# Careers on VaultOS

PineryZ is hiring people to build VaultOS — the on-chain vault foundation a business can offer under its own name and symbol. The public example is the [Ethereum Sepolia demonstration](/vaultos/demonstration).

These roles leave room for the yield platform that comes later. Each description is also published on the [Careers](https://pineryz.com/careers) section of the website.

## Open roles

- [Smart contract development](/vaultos/careers/smart-contract-development)
- [Backend development](/vaultos/careers/backend-development)
- [Frontend development](/vaultos/careers/frontend-development)
- [UI/UX design](/vaultos/careers/ui-ux-design)

## How to apply

Apply on the [PineryZ careers page](https://pineryz.com/careers). Each role there has its own application.
