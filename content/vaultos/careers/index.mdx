# Careers on VaultOS

PineryZ is hiring people to build VaultOS, the vault a business offers under its own name. The work starts from [the foundation](/vaultos/foundation) already running. The opening work is the [first release](/vaultos/use-a-vault): a controlled asset, configuration, an operator, a ledger, and a documented API.

Strategies, the controls a firm needs for its own clients, real-world assets, and an SDK come after that release. They are on [The build](/vaultos/build). They are not the opening work.

Each description is also published on the [Careers](https://pineryz.com/careers) section of the website.

## Open roles

- [Smart contract development](/vaultos/careers/smart-contract-development)
- [Backend development](/vaultos/careers/backend-development)
- [Frontend development](/vaultos/careers/frontend-development)
- [UI/UX design](/vaultos/careers/ui-ux-design)

## How to apply

Apply on the [PineryZ careers page](https://pineryz.com/careers). Each role there has its own application.
