# UI/UX design

You will design the flows a business uses to offer a vault under its own name, and the flows a holder uses to deposit and withdraw. The opening product is an operable vault. The screens have to make that scope clear.

You will design creation, configuration, the operator’s view, and the holder’s position. A screen states the action the product can perform. A strategy, custody by VaultOS, and a real-world asset are later work on [The build](/vaultos/build), and the interface does not present them as available.

## Responsibilities

- Shape the creation flow, so a business sets a name and a symbol and sees the vault listed only after the creation check.
- Design the operator’s controls for a fee, a cap, and a pause, separate from the holder’s signature to move assets.
- Design the holder’s view of a position: shares, the asset in the vault, and a deposit, withdrawal, or redemption.
- Work in Figma, and write the decisions down so the same pattern can be built again.
- Work with the engineers building the console, the API, and the contracts.

## Requirements

- Experience with product design for software interfaces.
- Familiarity with Figma.
- Ability to explain a flow clearly, in writing and in review.
- Comfort designing inside an existing product and extending the same pattern.
- Care for what a screen promises, and for keeping later work off the screens of the first release.
